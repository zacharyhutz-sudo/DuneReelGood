The house

Between the dunes and the Gulf.

Have coffee on the deck, grab a chair from the beach closet, and take the boardwalk down to the water. Back at the house, there’s space to cook dinner together and a second Gulf-view deck off the upstairs king suite.

DuneReelGood is a two-bedroom, two-bath end-unit vacation townhome in the gated Barrier Dunes community of Cape San Blas. It welcomes up to six guests, with a king suite upstairs, a queen bedroom with a private balcony on the main level, and a queen sleeper sofa in the living room.

From the boardwalk to the beach to the shared community pools and courts, the best parts of the Cape are close by. Explore our Cape San Blas local guide for nearby places to visit.

A closer look

Your stay in Barrier Dunes.

Settle into an end-unit beach townhome made for unhurried Gulf Coast days. Here is what to expect when you stay at DuneReelGood.

Room for six

Two bedrooms and two bathrooms, plus a queen sleeper sofa in the living area. The upstairs king suite opens to a second Gulf-view deck.

Beach days made easy

The beach is about a one-minute walk away using the community boardwalk. A beach cart, chairs, and a cooler are provided at the house.

Time together

Cook in the full kitchen, dine on the covered deck, or enjoy the community’s two pools and pickleball and tennis courts.
