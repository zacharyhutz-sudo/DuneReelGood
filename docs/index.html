The house

Between the dunes and the Gulf.

Have coffee on the deck, grab a chair from the beach closet, and take the boardwalk down to the water. Back at the house, there’s space to cook dinner together and a second Gulf-view deck off the upstairs king suite.

DuneReelGood is an end-unit townhome in gated Barrier Dunes. The main level has a queen bedroom with a private balcony, and the living room has a queen sleeper sofa.
